# Personal page rules

- Personal pages hold what I know that the team pages do not say yet.
- A twin page (frontmatter `twin_of`, same name as the team page) holds only additions to its team page. Never repeat the team page. It starts with the line `Team page: [name](../team/name.md)` under the title.
- Write in the style the section already uses: prose stays prose, a table gets a row, a list gets an item.
- End every statement with where it came from: `(src: <file or document name>)`.
- Tags: anything in `personal/tags.md` or `team/tags.md`.
- Mark guesses with #hunch and things to check with #verify, so they are never promoted by accident.
- Links may point to personal or team pages that exist.
- Contradictions go under the page's `## Conflicts` section with both versions and their sources. A page with no such section: leave the fact unfiled.
- Keep each page under 150 lines.
