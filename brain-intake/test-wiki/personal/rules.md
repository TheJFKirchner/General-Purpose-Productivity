# Personal page rules

- Personal pages hold what I know that the team pages do not say yet.
- A twin page (frontmatter `twin_of`) holds only additions to its team page. Never repeat the team page.
- Every fact is one bullet, one idea, with `(src: <record>)` at the end.
- Tags: anything in `personal/tags.md` or `team/tags.md`.
- Mark guesses with #hunch and things to check with #verify, so they are never promoted by accident.
- Wikilinks may point to personal or team pages that are in the page list.
- Contradictions go under `## Conflicts` with both versions. Never overwrite.
- Keep the sections a page already has. Add a new section only when nothing fits.
- Keep each page under 150 lines.
