Summary: Carrier summary of next year's stop-loss treaty terms for two clients. Adds laser details and a note on how the carrier treats the MLR offset.

## [[stop-loss]]
- One carrier now requires laser lists to be confirmed in writing 30 days before renewal.
- Laser removals at renewal are usually traded for a higher specific attachment point.

## [[medical-loss-ratio]] > Traps
- The carrier's own MLR report deducts stop-loss reimbursements, so it will not match the regulatory view.

## New page: lasering
- Lasering decisions are made by the carrier's underwriter at renewal, using the prior 12 months of large claims.
