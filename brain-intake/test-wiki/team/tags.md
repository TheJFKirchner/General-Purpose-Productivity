# Team tags

- #definition: the agreed meaning of a term
- #trap: a mistake people make with this data or metric
- #formula: a calculation people should copy exactly
- #table: a fact about a source table
- #metric: a fact about a reported metric
- #regulatory: driven by a regulator or contract requirement
- #actuarial: owned by or agreed with the actuarial team
- #source-system: about the upstream system that feeds the warehouse
- #reporting: about a standard report or its schedule
