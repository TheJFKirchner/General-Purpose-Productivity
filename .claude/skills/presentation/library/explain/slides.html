Approach

We compared matched groups of customers on the old and new setup

Step 1

Define the group

Customers who joined April to October.

Step 2

Match old and new

Same plan, region and company size.

Step 3

Compare churn

Cancelled within 90 days of joining.

Step 4

Check the reasons

Exit survey answers from those who left.

Data and validation

Two sources cover every customer who joined from April to October

What we used

Accounts

18,420 new customers, with join and cancel dates

Exit survey

1,112 answers from customers who cancelled

How we checked it

  • Customer counts match the finance report to within 0.2%
  • 312 duplicate accounts removed
  • Trial accounts left out, because they cannot cancel
Sources: customer accounts data and exit survey, April to October 2026.
