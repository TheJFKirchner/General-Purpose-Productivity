Goal

We set out to cut the share of new customers who cancel in their first 90 days

The problem

5.1% of new customers cancelled each month, most before finishing setup.

The change

A guided setup replaced the self-serve checklist in June.

How we judge it

Early churn at or below 3.5% a month by October.

"I gave up on day six. Nobody told me which step came next." Exit survey answer, August. "Setup took too long" is still 38% of the reasons customers give for leaving.
