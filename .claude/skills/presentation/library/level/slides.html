Trend by coverage

Bodily injury is rising 7.5% a year and accounts for half of the total trend

Reading the chart

  • Bodily injury loss cost rises 7.5% a year. The other coverages sit between 4.9% and 5.6%.
  • Bodily injury is 40% of losses, so it adds 3.0 of the 6.0 points of total trend.
  • Its cost per claim grows 9.5% a year: more claimants hire attorneys, and medical bills are higher.
  • Its claim frequency falls 1.8% a year, which offsets only a small part.

One trend for all coverages would underprice liability and overprice physical damage.

Annual change in loss cost per insured car, exponential fit to accident years 2021 to 2025. Bodily injury: injuries to other people that our policyholder is liable for. Comprehensive: theft, hail and other non-crash damage. Personal auto claims data as of December 2025.
