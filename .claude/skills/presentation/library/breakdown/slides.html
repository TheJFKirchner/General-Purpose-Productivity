Loss ratio bridge

Catastrophe claims added 11.5 points to the commercial property loss ratio in 2025

Without catastrophe claims the loss ratio would have risen 7.5 points, to 59.5%. Rate increases took 4.1 points off.

Loss ratio: incurred claims divided by earned premium, accident years 2024 and 2025 as of December 2025. Steps are points of loss ratio. Large losses are single claims above $250,000; attritional claims are those below it. Internal claims data.

Rate indication

The indicated rate change rose from 4.1% to 9.3%, and loss trend is the largest part

Reading the bridge

  • Loss trend adds 3.1 points: the selected severity trend moved from 5.0% to 7.5% a year.
  • Loss development adds 2.2 points because accident years 2021 to 2023 settled above their reserves.
  • Expenses add 0.5 points, and a shift toward lower-risk classes takes 0.6 points off.

Trend and development explain the whole increase. Expenses and mix cancel each other out.

Rate indication: the rate change the data says is needed to reach the target loss ratio. Homeowners, state filing review as of June 2025 against June 2024. Steps are points of indicated rate change. Actuarial rate review.

Book of business

Commercial lines grew from 18% to 27% of written premium in four years

  • Commercial premium almost doubled, from $256m to $502m.
  • Personal auto lost 8 points of share while its premium still grew 8%.

Commercial claims take longer to settle, so more of the reserve now rests on long-range estimates.

Share of gross written premium by line of business, 2021 ($1.42bn) and 2025 ($1.86bn). Other is marine, surety and specialty. Policy system.
