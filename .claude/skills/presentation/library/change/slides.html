Loss cost trend

Loss cost per car has risen 6.0% a year for three years with no sign of slowing

What this shows

  • The solid line is the loss cost per insured car over the latest 12 months, restated each quarter.
  • The dashed line is the fitted trend. No quarter sits more than 1% away from it.
  • At $832 per car, the latest year is 17% above the year to March 2023.

Rates set on a 4.0% trend fall further behind each quarter.

Loss cost (pure premium): incurred losses divided by insured car-years, all coverages, rolling 12 months to each quarter end. Fitted trend is an exponential fit to the 12 points. Personal auto claims data as of December 2025.

Fitting period

Collision repair costs now grow 4.4% a year, down from 8.5% over the full five years

Is the trend slowing?

  • The dots are the average collision claim. It climbed fastest in 2021 and 2022.
  • A fit to all five years gives 8.5% a year. A fit to the last two gives 4.4%.
  • Used car and parts prices stopped rising in 2024, so the recent pace is the better guide.

We select 5.5%, above the two-year fit, because repair labour rates are still rising.

Average incurred cost per collision claim, rolling 12 months to each half-year end, June 2021 to December 2025. Lines are exponential fits to all ten points and to the last four. Personal auto claims data as of December 2025.

Excluded period

Leaving out 2020 turns a rising frequency trend into a falling one

Lockdowns cut driving in 2020 and claims fell by a quarter. With 2020 in the fit, frequency appears to rise 0.9% a year. Without it, frequency falls 1.0% a year.

Reported claims per 100 insured car-years, all coverages, accident years 2019 to 2025. Trends are exponential fits. Personal auto claims data as of December 2025.

Frequency and severity

Cost per claim rose 7.1% a year while frequency fell 1.0%

Frequency: claims per 100 cars

Severity: average cost per claim

  • Frequency drifted down from 14.6 to 14.0 claims per 100 cars over four years.
  • Severity rose every year, from $4,510 to $5,940 per claim.
  • Together they give the 6.0% loss cost trend. All of the increase comes from severity.
Frequency: reported claims per 100 insured car-years. Severity: incurred loss per claim. All coverages, accident years 2021 to 2025. Both charts are drawn to the same relative scale. Personal auto claims data as of December 2025.
