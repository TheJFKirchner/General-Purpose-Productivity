Reserve position

The carried reserve sits in the upper half of the range of reasonable estimates

  • The range runs from 7% below the central estimate to 10% above it.
  • It is wider on the high side because liability claims can keep growing for years after they are reported.
  • The carried reserve is $82m below the high end and $118m above the low end.

If the central estimate rises by more than $36m, the margin becomes a shortfall.

Range of reasonable estimates: the span of results from actuarial methods and assumptions we consider reasonable. $ millions, net of reinsurance, at 30 September 2026.

Risks and caveats

The shortfall could be $32M or $64M, and every scenario needs an increase

What could move the number

  • Twelve large auto claims are under a year old. If they settle at typical values, the gap is $32M.
  • We assume auto claim costs keep rising 9% a year. At 11%, the gap is $56M.
  • A further 3 months of court delay in general liability adds $8M, which gives $64M.
  • Two years of data support the 9% trend, so we will test it again each quarter.

Approving $42M now is the middle of the range, not the cautious end.
