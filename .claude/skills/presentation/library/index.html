Open and frame

Set up what the audience is about to hear.

FR-01

title

Large headline, kicker above, one line below. No visual.

  • Opening with the answer or the subject
  • Telling the audience who is presenting and when

Related: FR-02 FR-05

FR-02

section-divider

Section name large, one line saying what the section will show.

  • Marking a change of topic in a longer deck
  • Giving the audience a breath before a new line of argument

Related: FR-01 FR-05

FR-03

agenda-rows

Numbered rows: part name and one line each.

  • Showing the route through a longer deck
  • Matching what is said to the sections in the bottom bar

Related: DE-02 DE-03

FR-04

goal-three-cards

Three cards side by side, the third tinted.

  • Reminding the audience of the original goal
  • Laying out problem, action and measure of success in one view
  • Any three parallel ideas where the last one matters most

Related: CP-08 DE-05 EX-02

FR-05

statement-quote

One sentence or quote in large type, source line under it.

  • Making the audience hear one message with nothing competing
  • Letting a customer or stakeholder say it in their own words

Related: FR-01 FR-02
