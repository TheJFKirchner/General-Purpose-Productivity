Example of the tone we want (illustrative only, not real figures):

- Spend rose faster than membership this month, so the increase is per-member cost, not growth.
- Most of the change sits in two areas; everything else moved within normal month-to-month noise.
- The rise is concentrated in a few high-cost cases rather than a broad shift, which makes it less likely to persist.

Lead with the answer. One idea per bullet. Name the driver, give the size, stop. No adjectives like "significant" or "notable" unless a number backs them. Avoid jargon the committee would not use themselves.
