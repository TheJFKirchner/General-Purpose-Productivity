Feature card grid

.cards is a grid that fills the row and wraps on small screens; add .three for exactly three across on wide screens, or .two for wider cards. Flow diagram (inline SVG)

Boxes and arrows drawn in the page, coloured by cosmo.css, so they follow light and dark. Wrap in <figure class="flow"><div class="flow-box">. Each box is a <g class="node"> holding a rect and one or two text lines (second line class="sub"; text is centred on its x, y). Box kinds: .node plain, .node.key the main one (blue), .node.soft shared or supporting (pale blue), .node.ghost optional (dashed). Arrows are <path class="edge"> (.dash dashed, .move gently moving) ending with marker-end; .label is small arrow text; .group and .group-label draw a dashed area around boxes.

Tips: keep the viewBox about 720 wide (it scrolls sideways inside its box on phones); 15px main text fits about 8 characters per 60 units of box width; give each box at least 40 units of gap for its arrow; arrows end 4 units short of the box they point at.

Placeholder card: "coming soon"

A card for something not written yet: dashed, hatched and greyed so nobody mistakes it for finished. Add .ph to a .card and start it with a .tag.soon. When it is written, remove ph and the tag. Duplicate freely inside a .cards grid.
