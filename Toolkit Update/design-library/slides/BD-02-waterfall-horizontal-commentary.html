The indicated rate change rose from 4.1% to 9.3%, and loss trend is the largest part

Indicated rate change, June 2024 to June 2025

  • Loss trend adds 3.1 points: the selected severity trend moved from 5.0% to 7.5% a year.
  • Loss development adds 2.2 points because accident years 2021 to 2023 settled above their reserves.
  • Expenses add 0.5 points, and a shift toward lower-risk classes takes 0.6 points off.

Trend and development explain the whole increase. Expenses and mix cancel each other out.
