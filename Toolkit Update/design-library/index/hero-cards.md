# Design library: hero cards

id | type | name | purpose | data it needs (AUTHORING.md, "Adding an item to a project")

- HC-01 | hero card | plan-tiles | Supporting measures as tiles against plan: the value, a bar to the plan tick and last year, a status top bar and note. | data: label (text, one); value (text, one); status (text, one); actual-bar (number, one); plan-bar (number, one); last-bar (number, optional); verdict (text, one); plan (text, one)
- HC-02 | hero card | spotlight-tick | The page's one lead measure against its target: a large figure with its gap, a sentence saying why, a bar to the target tick and the basis; use it for the headline figure of a report or dashboard, such as loss ratio against target. | data: label (figure, one); value (figure, one); gap (figure, one); status (figure, one); said (figure, optional); actual-bar (figure, one); target-bar (figure, one); last-bar (figure, optional); scale-low (figure, one); scale-high (figure, one); verdict (figure, optional); ticks (figure, optional); basis (figure, optional)
- HC-03 | hero card | split-strip | A figure with the parts that make it up, as one share strip and a key of values and shares; use it when a total such as PMPM or member months needs its split by service category or product beside it. | data: label (text, one); value (text, one); change (text, one); status (text, one); compare (text, optional); basis (text, optional)
- HC-04 | hero card | recent-range | A figure placed within its own recent range, as a marker on a track with the range shaded; use it for utilisation per 1,000 or any rate where the question is whether this period is unusual for it. | data: label (text, one); value (text, one); change (text, one); status (text, one); said (text, optional); range-left (number, one); range-width (number, one); now (number, one); scale-low (text, one); scale-high (text, one); range-key (text, optional); now-key (text, optional); basis (text, optional)
- HC-05 | hero card | streak-months | How long a result has persisted, as a run of months marked favourable or unfavourable under the current streak; use it when the point is that claims have run above expected, or under target, for several months in a row. | data: label (text, one); value (text, one); count (text, one); status (text, one); basis (text, optional)
- HC-06 | hero card | rank-dots | Where one group stands among its peers, as its figure and a numbered row of rank positions with its own filled; use it when a region, product or plan needs to be placed against the others, such as second-highest PMPM of six regions. | data: label (text, one); value (text, one); change (text, one); status (text, one); rank (text, one); note (text, optional); basis (text, optional)
- HC-07 | hero card | driver-sentence | One sentence naming the biggest driver of a change, on the band colour, with a strip showing its share of the total; use it beside the figure cards when the reader's next question is what caused the movement. | data: label (text, one); said (text, one); share (number, one); share-label (text, one); compare (text, optional); note (text, optional); basis (text, optional); status (text, one)
- HC-08 | hero card | figure-trend-slate | A figure with its change on last year and a small this-year-against-last-year line under it in the same card; use it on a dashboard when the latest month's PMPM or utilisation needs its path shown, not just its level. | data: label (text, one); value (text, one); change (text, one); status (text, one); basis (text, optional)
- HC-09 | hero card | card-row-basis | A row of four small figure cards that work as a set at the top of a report or dashboard, each with its change and good direction, sharing one basis line; use it for the opening summary of member months, PMPM, loss ratio and completion. | data: label (text, one); value (text, one); change (text, one); status (text, one); compare (text, one); direction (text, optional)
- HC-10 | hero card | trend-split-three | A PMPM trend with its split into unit cost, utilisation and mix as three small signed bars on one scale; use it when the reader needs to see which of the three moved the cost trend, and whether any part pulled the other way. | data: label (text, one); value (text, one); compare (text, one); status (text, one); check (text, optional); basis (text, optional)
- HC-11 | hero card | estimate-range-completion | A best estimate of incurred claims as paid to date plus IBNR, with its low-to-high range and how complete the period is; use it to report an ultimate or reserve estimate so the reader sees both the figure and how much of it is still estimated. | data: label (text, one); value (text, one); change (text, one); status (text, one); paid-bar (number, one); ibnr-left (number, one); ibnr-bar (number, one); range-left (number, one); range-width (number, one); best (number, one); scale-high (text, one); paid-key (text, one); ibnr-key (text, one); range-key (text, one); complete (text, one); complete-note (text, optional); basis (text, optional)
- HC-12 | hero card | volume-cost-share | A count over a threshold set against its share of members and its share of total cost, as two bars on one scale; use it for large claimants over a pooling point, or any few cases that carry a large part of a total. | data: label (text, one); value (text, one); change (text, one); status (text, one); member-share (number, one); cost-share (number, one); last-share (number, optional); note (text, optional); basis (text, optional)
- HC-13 | hero card | build-up-rows | A proposed rate change with the three drivers that build it as signed bars from a zero line, checked against the indicated rate; use it in a renewal readout so the underwriter sees the final figure and what made it in one card. | data: label (text, one); value (text, one); against (text, one); status (text, one); basis (text, optional)
- HC-14 | hero card | ratio-centre-bar | An actual-to-expected ratio as a bar from 1.00 with the band of normal variation for its credibility, and a plain-words reading; use it to say whether experience really ran above or below the pricing basis or is within chance. | data: label (text, one); value (text, one); change (text, one); status (text, one); reading (text, optional); band-left (number, one); band-width (number, one); bar-left (number, one); bar-width (number, one); band-key (text, optional); basis (text, optional)
- HC-15 | hero card | figure-tiles | Use to lead with a few headline figures: plain tiles, each a label, a big figure and a note line, with an optional status colour on the top bar. | data: label (text, one); value (number, one); note (text, optional); status (text, optional)
