# Design library: charts

id | type | name | purpose | data it needs (AUTHORING.md, "Adding an item to a project")

- CT-02 | chart | bars-ranked | Use to rank items on one measure when the labels are long, or to show which item drives a result, such as PMPM trend by service category; draws horizontal bars sorted largest first, the leader in blue, values at the bar ends and the overall figure as a reference line. | data: label (text, one); value (number, one); reference (figure, optional)
- CT-04 | chart | line-fit | Use to show a steady rate of change, or how closely actuals follow a fitted or expected path, such as quarterly PMPM against a fitted annual trend; draws the actual line in blue, the latest period dashed as an estimate, and a dashed grey fitted line, both ends labelled. | data: period (text, one); actual (number, one); fitted (number, optional); estimate (number, optional)
- CT-06 | chart | waterfall | Use to bridge from a starting figure to an ending figure by cause, or to show which driver accounts for most of a change, such as prior to current PMPM by trend component; draws a vertical bridge with the totals dark grey, the step that matters in blue and the other steps light grey. | data: step (text, one); base (number, one); total (number, one); rise (number, optional); fall (number, optional); key (number, optional)
- CT-07 | chart | target-tick-bars | Use to show each item against its own target or plan and pick out the one item the reader must look at, a miss or a beat, such as loss ratio by product against target; draws horizontal bars with a dark tick at each target, the chosen item in blue with its value inside the bar. | data: label (text, one); value (number, one); target (number, one)
- CT-08 | chart | dumbbell | Use to show movement between two points for several items at once, and which moved most and which way, such as loss ratio by product last year against this year to date; draws two dots per item joined by a short bar, the earlier grey, the later dark, the item that matters in blue. | data: label (text, one); start (number, one); end (number, one); low (number, one); gap (number, one)
- CT-10 | chart | heatmap-grid | Use to show a pattern across two dimensions or point at one part of a grid of figures, such as the latest diagonal of a claims development triangle; draws a small grid of figures on light grey cells, the cells that matter in blue with paper-coloured text. | data: column (text, one); row (text, one); value (number, one); flag (number, one)
- CT-11 | chart | range-bar | Use to show where a selected figure sits within a reasonable range and how wide the uncertainty is, such as the IBNR reserve against its low-to-high range; draws one grey bar from low to high on a value axis, the central estimate as a dashed line and the selected estimate as a solid blue line. | data: label (text, one); low (number, one); range (number, one); central (figure, optional); selected (figure, optional)
- CT-12 | chart | year-overlay-lines | Use to read seasonality and the year-over-year gap at once, such as PMPM by incurred month for three years; draws one line per year across the calendar months, the current year in blue with its incomplete months dashed and the earlier years in greys. | data: month (text, one); prior (number, one or more); current (number, one); estimate (number, optional)
- CT-13 | chart | product-panels | Use to compare several products over time without a tangle of lines, such as PMPM actual against expected by quarter; draws one small panel per product on a shared scale, actual against expected in each, with the A/E ratio in the panel title and the line coloured by whether it is over, under or within tolerance. | data: quarter (text, one); expected-1 (number, one); actual-1 (number, one); latest-1 (number, one); expected-2 (number, optional); actual-2 (number, optional); latest-2 (number, optional); expected-3 (number, optional); actual-3 (number, optional); latest-3 (number, optional)
- CT-14 | chart | bars-rate-dual-axis | Use to show two measures of different units for the same groups, such as PMPM and trend by product against the pricing trend; draws two side-by-side panels on one product axis, each with its own titled value axis, and puts every group past the reference in blue in both panels. | data: label (text, one); level (number, one); change (number, one); reference (figure, optional)
- CT-15 | chart | trend-decomp-bars | Use to show what drives each product's trend and where the drivers offset; draws horizontal bars per product split into unit cost, utilisation and mix, increases right of zero and decreases left, a tick at the net and the net trend in a right-hand column. | data: label (text, one); driver (number, one or more); net (number, one); net-label (text, one)
- CT-16 | chart | development-curves | Use to show how fast claims complete and whether recent months are paying slower or faster than a complete month; draws one cumulative curve per incurral month against months of run-out, a complete month as the grey reference, recent months stopping where their data ends and the latest projected to ultimate as a dashed line. | data: age (text, one); reference (number, one); projected (number, optional); recent (number, any); latest (number, one)
- CT-17 | chart | ae-range-rows | Use to show actual against expected by segment over time, fairly across segments of different size; draws one row per segment with its expected range as a grey bar around 100%, the last three quarters as hollow dots, the current quarter as a dashed estimate marker, and any value outside the range as a labelled diamond in the favourable or unfavourable colour. | data: label (text, one); low (number, one); range (number, one); quarter (number, one); estimate (number, optional); above (number, optional); below (number, optional)
- CT-18 | chart | cost-use-bubbles | Use to see which service categories drive one product's trend through price, through use, or both; draws bubbles placed by change in utilisation and change in unit cost, sized by PMPM with the PMPM printed on each label, quadrant labels and a diagonal at the pricing trend, the categories past it in blue. | data: x (number, one); y (number, one); size (number, one); name (text, one)
- CT-19 | chart | mix-shift-columns | Use to show how the make-up of spend changes over time, or that a result changed because the mix changed (two columns for two points), such as service category mix of PMPM; draws 100% stacked columns across several years, the shifting segment at the base in blue, the rest grey, shares inside, names at the right and the total level under each year. | data: period (text, one); part (number, one or more)
- CT-20 | chart | target-band-lines | Use to show a rate against its target or assumption over time, such as loss ratio by product against target, or the effect of a change against a group that did not change; draws several lines against a shaded target range and a thin target line, the line that matters in blue, an event marked, the latest period dashed as an estimate and each line labelled at its end. | data: period (text, one); group (number, one or more); estimate (number, any); target (figure, optional)
- CT-21 | chart | paid-ibnr-bars | Use for a monthly measure whose recent months are not yet complete, such as incurred PMPM with IBNR; draws bars by incurred month with the paid amount solid and the estimated unreported amount as a dashed, lighter cap, the total above each bar and last year as a dashed line. | data: month (text, one); paid (number, one); ibnr (number, one); prior (number, optional)
- CT-22 | chart | plan-drift-steps | Use to show when a gap to budget started to open and how large it has grown; draws bars for each month's amount over or under budget, shaded by direction, with the running total as a dark step line, dashed where it includes an estimate, and its year-to-date figure and percent of budget labelled at the end. | data: month (text, one); variance (number, one); running (number, one); estimate (number, optional)
- CT-23 | chart | variance-split | Use to show which groups are over or under plan and by how much, in dollars and as a percent of plan; draws horizontal bars around a zero line, over in the warning colour extending right, under in the favourable colour extending left, sorted, with the total as a last row. | data: label (text, one); variance (number, one)
- CT-24 | chart | pareto-bars | Use to show that a few drivers explain most of a total, such as large-claimant spend by condition; draws ranked bars with the leading few in blue, the claimant count under each name, and a running-share line on a second axis with an 80% reference. | data: label (text, one); value (number, one); share (number, one); cut (figure, optional)
- CT-27 | chart | state-tiles | Use to compare states fairly whatever their area, such as PMPM trend by state, without reading noise from small states; draws equal-size tiles laid out like a map of the US states, each shaded by its value in four steps of one colour, low-volume states outlined and unshaded, beside a readout of the overall, highest and lowest. | data: col (number, one); row (number, one); value (number, one); state (text, one); label (text, one); small (number, optional)
- CT-28 | chart | capsule-count | Use to show that a small share of members carries a large share of spend; draws two blocks of 100 capsules side by side, one for members and one for allowed dollars, the high-cost group in blue in both, with a large figure under each. | data: col (number, one); row (number, one); part (number, one); col-2 (number, optional); row-2 (number, optional); part-2 (number, optional)
- CT-29 | chart | rate-buildup-bridge | Use to walk a renewal from experience to the required premium and set it against the current premium and the proposed rate action; draws a PMPM bridge through trend, plan changes, the credibility blend with manual, administration and margin, with the current and proposed premiums as reference lines. | data: step (text, one); base (number, one); total (number, one); rise (number, optional); fall (number, optional); key (number, optional); current (figure, optional); required (figure, optional)
- CT-30 | chart | rolling-12-panels | Use to show the run rate of a cost and its trend without the noise of single months, such as rolling 12-month PMPM and rolling 12-month trend; draws two stacked panels on one month axis, the level above and the trend percent below against the pricing trend, the months not yet complete shaded and dashed. | data: month (text, one); level (number, one); level-estimate (number, optional); trend (number, one); trend-estimate (number, optional); reference (figure, optional)
- CT-31 | chart | tornado-fan | Use to show how uncertain a projection is and which assumptions move it most, such as projected PMPM for next year; draws a sensitivity tornado of each assumption's effect on the central estimate beside a fan of the projection with 50% and 90% ranges around the dashed central line. | data: assumption (text, one); low (number, one); high (number, one); quarter (text, one); fan-base (number, one); fan-outer (number, one); fan-inner (number, one); actual (number, one); projected (number, one)
- CT-32 | chart | util-per-1000-panels | Use to compare utilisation per 1,000 members by service category against last year and a benchmark, each in its own unit; draws one small panel per category with last year and this year to date as bars, the benchmark as a line, and the change on each in the panel title, categories more than 5% over benchmark in blue. | data: period (text, one); value-1 (number, one); benchmark-1 (figure, optional); value-2 (number, optional); benchmark-2 (figure, optional); value-3 (number, optional); benchmark-3 (figure, optional); value-4 (number, optional); benchmark-4 (figure, optional)
- CT-33 | chart | stop-loss-excess | Use to show how often and how far large claimants pierce the specific stop-loss attachment, year by year, against the expected excess; draws bars of excess dollars above the attachment with the claimant count under each year and the expected excess as a dashed line, the latest year dashed as an estimate. | data: label (text, one); excess (number, one); expected (number, optional)
- CT-34 | chart | plain-bars | Use for the plainest comparison of one or more measures across groups or periods: vertical bars with value labels, one colour per measure, a legend naming them. | data: label (text, one); value (number, one or more)
- CT-35 | chart | plain-line | Use for one or more measures over time: plain lines, each named at its end, no highlight. | data: label (text, one); value (number, one or more)
- CT-36 | chart | plain-stacked-bars | Use to show how parts make up a total across groups or periods: plain stacked bars, one colour per part, a legend naming them. | data: label (text, one); part (number, one or more)
- CT-37 | chart | plain-scatter | Use to show how two measures move together across items: plain dots on two value axes, each dot named. | data: x (number, one); y (number, one); name (text, one)
