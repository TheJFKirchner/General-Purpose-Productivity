# Design library: animations

id | type | name | purpose

- AN-01 | animation | lead-count-up | A named measure (medical PMPM, year to date) counts up, its bar grows towards the plan tick and its monthly trend against last year draws in, so the reader takes in the figure, what it is measured against and its basis; the kit's three motions in one exhibit.
- AN-02 | animation | rate-build-steps | A rate build-up waterfall whose bars land one at a time, from experience claims through trend, benefit changes and pooling to projected claims, then admin and margin to the required premium, set against current premium so the rate action is measured on the right base; use to walk a renewal audience through the build step by step.
- AN-03 | animation | year-stepper | Monthly PMPM for one year against the year before, stepping 2022 to 2024 once on load or by the year buttons, so the line moves up while its seasonal shape stays visible; use for seasonality and year over year.
- AN-04 | animation | projection-extend | A monthly PMPM line that draws in to the latest actual month, pauses, then extends as a dashed projection with its range opening behind it, against a monthly plan line and a full-year projected-against-plan readout, so the reader sees where actuals stop and compares like with like; use for year-end projections.
- AN-05 | animation | triangle-fill | A paid claims development triangle that fills in one calendar diagonal at a time and ends on the latest diagonal highlighted, so the reader sees that each new month of payments adds one diagonal; use to explain lag triangles, run-out and IBNR.
- AN-06 | animation | focus-recede | A plain bar chart in which, after a beat, one bar stays in colour and the rest recede to grey while the finding appears, so the room looks at the one category that matters; use when one category drives a change.
- AN-07 | animation | basis-morph | Monthly claims by incurred month that start as paid to date, where recent months look low, then the IBNR estimate rises on top of each month to the completed view, so the reader sees the dip is unpaid claims, not lower cost; use when explaining paid against incurred and IBNR.
- AN-08 | animation | stack-regroup | A stacked column chart whose blocks slide from columns by product into columns by region, each block keeping its colour and label, so the reader follows the same pieces into the new grouping; use when one total has to be read two ways.
- AN-09 | animation | slide-steps | A slide that reveals its headline, then its chart, then each of its two points on a step (Space or Enter, a click or the Next step button; it plays through once on load), so a presenter can talk to one thing at a time; everything shows at once when motion is off.
- AN-10 | animation | total-split | A PMPM trend that counts up as one bar and then splits into unit cost, utilisation and mix, each part sized by its share and named underneath, so the reader sees the total first and then what it is made of; use for trend decomposition.
