Slides

Open and frame

Set up what the audience is about to hear.

FR-01

title

Large headline, kicker above, one line below. No visual.

  • Opening with the answer or the subject
  • Telling the audience who is presenting and when

Related: FR-02 FR-05

FR-02

section-divider

Section name large, one line saying what the section will show.

  • Marking a change of topic in a longer deck
  • Giving the audience a breath before a new line of argument

Related: FR-01 FR-05

FR-03

agenda-rows

Numbered rows: part name and one line each.

  • Showing the route through a longer deck
  • Matching what is said to the sections in the bottom bar

Related: DE-02 DE-03

FR-04

goal-three-cards

Three cards side by side, the third tinted.

  • Reminding the audience of the original goal
  • Laying out problem, action and measure of success in one view
  • Any three parallel ideas where the last one matters most

Related: CP-08 DE-05 EX-02

FR-05

statement-quote

One sentence or quote in large type, source line under it.

  • Making the audience hear one message with nothing competing
  • Letting a customer or stakeholder say it in their own words

Related: FR-01 FR-02

Explain how the work was done

Give the audience enough method to trust the result.

EX-01

steps-flow

Three to five steps on a line, each with a title and one line.

  • Showing a method or process in order
  • Showing a timeline or plan with the current point marked
  • Giving a non-technical audience the approach in ten seconds

Related: DE-06 DE-07

EX-02

data-two-cards

Two cards: what was used (rows) and how it was checked (bullets).

  • Showing what the analysis rests on and why it can be trusted
  • Answering scope and data-quality questions before they are asked

Related: FR-04

Show where a number stands

One figure, or a few, and what they are measured against.

LV-01

stats-three

Three large figures in a row, each with a label and a comparison note, one sentence under them. No chart.

  • Landing two or three results at once
  • Showing results against target or prior without a chart

Related: DE-01 LV-04 BD-06

LV-02

hero-number-line

One very large figure with its meaning in plain words, a line chart beside it.

  • Making one number the whole message
  • Showing the figure and the path that led to it

Related: LV-03 BD-07

LV-03

hero-number-quote

One very large figure beside a one-sentence statement.

  • Closing on the one thing to remember
  • Pairing a figure with the sentence that explains it

Related: LV-02 BD-07

LV-04

stats-over-bars

Three figure cards across the top, a bar chart and two bullets under.

  • Stating a position with its two inputs and the gap between them
  • Showing the headline figures and how one of them has moved

Related: LV-01 DE-01 BD-06

LV-05

bars-highlight-commentary

Vertical bars with one highlighted, commentary bullets and a so-what line on the right.

  • Showing which category stands out
  • Comparing a handful of groups on one measure and saying why one differs

Related: LV-06 CH-04 CH-05

LV-06

bars-ranked-commentary

Sorted horizontal bars with the leader highlighted, fuller commentary on the right.

  • Ranking items when the labels are long
  • Showing which item drives a result
  • A slide meant to be read without a presenter

Show change over time

How something moved, and whether the movement is real.

CH-01

line-fit-commentary

Actual line with a dashed fitted line, commentary and so-what on the right.

  • Showing a steady rate of change
  • Showing how closely actuals follow a fitted or expected path

Related: CH-02 CH-03

CH-02

line-event-callout

Callout with one figure and three bullets on the left; two lines with an event marked on the right.

  • Showing the effect of a change against a group that did not change
  • Tying a movement to the moment that caused it

Related: CH-01 CH-03 CP-03

CH-03

dots-two-fits

Commentary on the left; actual points with a longer and a shorter fitted line on the right.

  • Asking whether a rate of change is speeding up or slowing
  • Comparing a long-run view with a recent view of the same measure

Related: CH-01 CH-02

CH-04

bars-outlier-callout

One callout sentence over full-width bars with one period highlighted.

  • Calling out an unusual period and how it was treated
  • A lean slide for a senior audience: one sentence, one picture

Related: LV-05 LV-06 CH-05

CH-05

bars-before-after

Callout with the size of the change and two bullets; bars by period with the later periods highlighted.

  • Showing a level shift after a change
  • Stating the size of an effect next to the evidence for it

Related: LV-05 LV-06 CH-04

CH-06

two-charts-bullets

Two small line charts side by side, one muted and one highlighted, three bullets in a row under them.

  • Splitting a result into two measures to show which one is moving
  • Showing that one thing is stable while another is not

Related: CP-04

CH-07

change-cards-bars

Three stacked cards, each a change with its size and direction, beside a bar chart of the total at each review.

  • Saying what is different since the audience last saw this
  • Listing a few changes with their sizes alongside the overall movement

Related: CH-05

CP-03

two-lines-fuller-commentary

Fuller commentary on the left, two lines over time on the right.

  • Comparing our experience with a benchmark or peer

Related: CP-04 CH-02

CP-04

two-lines-choice-cards

Two lines over time, then one card per alternative with the chosen one tinted.

  • Showing two approaches that give different answers and which was chosen

Related: CP-03 CH-02 CH-06

CP-05

dumbbell-callout

Full-width dumbbell chart (two points joined per category), one sentence under it.

Related: CP-01 CP-02

CP-06

two-numbers-difference

Two large figures in cards with the difference stated between them. No chart.

  • A before and after, or an old and new, in one glance

Related: LV-01

CP-07

table-key-row-commentary

Small table with one highlighted row, commentary and so-what beside it.

  • Showing plan, actual and difference where exact figures will be read

CP-08

option-cards-recommendation

Two cards comparing options on the same rows, then the recommendation. No chart.
