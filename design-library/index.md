# Design library index

One line per item: id, type, name, purpose. An item is the file `<type folder>/<id>-<name>.html`; open only the one you need.

## Slides (`slides/`)

- FR-01 | slide | title | Large headline, kicker above, one line below, no visual; for opening with the answer or the subject, or telling the audience who is presenting and when.
- FR-02 | slide | section-divider | Section name large, one line saying what the section will show; for marking a change of topic in a longer deck, or giving the audience a breath before a new line of argument.
- FR-03 | slide | agenda-rows | Numbered rows: part name and one line each; for showing the route through a longer deck, or matching what is said to the sections in the bottom bar.
- FR-04 | slide | goal-three-cards | Three cards side by side, the third tinted; for reminding the audience of the original goal, or laying out problem, action and measure of success in one view.
- FR-05 | slide | statement-quote | One sentence or quote in large type, source line under it; for making the audience hear one message with nothing competing, or letting a customer or stakeholder say it in their own words.
- EX-01 | slide | steps-flow | Three to five steps on a line, each with a title and one line; for showing a method or process in order, or showing a timeline or plan with the current point marked.
- EX-02 | slide | data-two-cards | Two cards: what was used (rows) and how it was checked (bullets); for showing what the analysis rests on and why it can be trusted, or answering scope and data-quality questions before they are asked.
- LV-01 | slide | stats-three | Three large figures in a row, each with a label and a comparison note, one sentence under them, no chart; for landing two or three results at once, or showing results against target or prior without a chart.
- LV-02 | slide | hero-number-line | One very large figure with its meaning in plain words, a line chart beside it; for making one number the whole message, or showing the figure and the path that led to it.
- LV-03 | slide | hero-number-quote | One very large figure beside a one-sentence statement; for closing on the one thing to remember, or pairing a figure with the sentence that explains it.
- LV-04 | slide | stats-over-bars | Three figure cards across the top, a bar chart and two bullets under; for stating a position with its two inputs and the gap between them, or showing the headline figures and how one of them has moved.
- LV-05 | slide | bars-highlight-commentary | Vertical bars with one highlighted, commentary bullets and a so-what line on the right; for showing which category stands out, or comparing a handful of groups on one measure and saying why one differs.
- LV-06 | slide | bars-ranked-commentary | Sorted horizontal bars with the leader highlighted, fuller commentary on the right; for ranking items when the labels are long, or showing which item drives a result.
- CH-01 | slide | line-fit-commentary | Actual line with a dashed fitted line, commentary and so-what on the right; for showing a steady rate of change, or showing how closely actuals follow a fitted or expected path.
- CH-02 | slide | line-event-callout | Callout with one figure and three bullets on the left; two lines with an event marked on the right; for showing the effect of a change against a group that did not change, or tying a movement to the moment that caused it.
- CH-03 | slide | dots-two-fits | Commentary on the left; actual points with a longer and a shorter fitted line on the right; for asking whether a rate of change is speeding up or slowing, or comparing a long-run view with a recent view of the same measure.
- CH-04 | slide | bars-outlier-callout | One callout sentence over full-width bars with one period highlighted; for calling out an unusual period and how it was treated, or a lean slide for a senior audience: one sentence, one picture.
- CH-05 | slide | bars-before-after | Callout with the size of the change and two bullets; bars by period with the later periods highlighted; for showing a level shift after a change, or stating the size of an effect next to the evidence for it.
- CH-06 | slide | two-charts-bullets | Two small line charts side by side, one muted and one highlighted, three bullets in a row under them; for splitting a result into two measures to show which one is moving, or showing that one thing is stable while another is not.
- CH-07 | slide | change-cards-bars | Three stacked cards, each a change with its size and direction, beside a bar chart of the total at each review; for saying what is different since the audience last saw this, or listing a few changes with their sizes alongside the overall movement.
- CP-01 | slide | paired-bars-commentary | Paired bars by category (two series), commentary and so-what on the right; for comparing actual with expected across a few groups, or showing where two measures agree and where they part.
- CP-02 | slide | bars-target-tick | Horizontal bars with a tick marking the target for each, the miss highlighted; commentary on the right; for showing performance against a target or plan for each item, or picking out the one item that missed.
- CP-03 | slide | two-lines-fuller-commentary | Fuller commentary on the left, two lines over time on the right; for comparing our experience with a benchmark or peer, or a comparison meant to be read without a presenter.
- CP-04 | slide | two-lines-choice-cards | Two lines over time, then one card per alternative with the chosen one tinted; for showing two approaches that give different answers and which was chosen, or explaining a judgement call between two views.
- CP-05 | slide | dumbbell-callout | Full-width dumbbell chart (two points joined per category), one sentence under it; for showing movement between two points for several items at once, or showing which items moved most and in which direction.
- CP-06 | slide | two-numbers-difference | Two large figures in cards with the difference stated between them, no chart; for a before and after, or an old and new, in one glance, or when the difference is the message and a chart would add nothing.
- CP-07 | slide | table-key-row-commentary | Small table with one highlighted row, commentary and so-what beside it; for showing plan, actual and difference where exact figures will be read, or any small table where one row carries the point.
- CP-08 | slide | option-cards-recommendation | Two cards comparing options on the same rows, then the recommendation, no chart; for putting two options side by side on cost, benefit and risk, or leading the audience to a recommendation they can see the basis for.
- BD-01 | slide | waterfall-callout | Full-width vertical waterfall from one figure to another, one-sentence callout under it; for bridging from a starting figure to an ending figure by cause, or showing which driver accounts for most of a change.
- BD-02 | slide | waterfall-horizontal-commentary | Horizontal waterfall on the left, fuller commentary on the right; for building up a result from its components when labels are long, or a bridge that needs explaining step by step, readable without a presenter.
- BD-03 | slide | number-small-waterfall | Callout with one large figure and two bullets on the left, a three-bar waterfall on the right; for stating the effect of one decision and showing where it lands, or a simple from, change, to story.
- BD-04 | slide | rows-plus-bars | Three numbered rows on the left, the same three effects as bars on the right; for splitting a change into a few named effects, or giving each effect a sentence and showing their relative size.
- BD-05 | slide | stacked-mix-shift | Two 100% stacked bars, bullets and a callout under them; for showing how the make-up of a total shifted between two points, or showing that a result changed because the mix changed.
- BD-06 | slide | stats-over-stacked-bars | Three figures across the top, stacked horizontal bars by category, commentary beside; for splitting a total into two factors across several categories, or showing the headline figures and which category is behind them.
- BD-07 | slide | hero-number-donut | One very large figure in a callout with a two-part doughnut, no commentary; for showing that one part explains most of the whole, or a lean slide where a single share is the message.
- BD-08 | slide | table-bars-sensitivity | Small table and two bullets on the left, bars for each scenario on the right; for showing what the result becomes under different assumptions, or giving exact figures and their shape together.
- UN-01 | slide | range-bar-commentary | One low-to-high range bar with the central and held figures marked, commentary under it; for showing where a chosen figure sits within a reasonable range, or making the size of the uncertainty visible.
- UN-02 | slide | scenario-bars-caveats | Scenario bars on the left, four caveats and a so-what line on the right; for telling the audience what could change the answer before they decide, or sizing a few scenarios against the central view.
- DE-01 | slide | summary-stats-ask | Answer as the headline, three large figures, the ask in a strip along the bottom; for an executive summary on one slide, or giving the answer, its support and the ask together.
- DE-02 | slide | numbered-findings | Numbered rows, each a finding with its figure on the right; for listing the takeaways with their sizes, or listing risks or issues ranked by size.
- DE-03 | slide | audience-rows | One row per audience group: who, what it means for them, the one figure; for translating a result for each group in the room, or showing each team what changes for them.
- DE-04 | slide | scorecard-status | Table with a status dot per row, one row highlighted, one line under it; for giving the status of several items at a glance, or showing which item needs attention.
- DE-05 | slide | decision-two-paths | The decision in a callout, then one card for what happens if approved and one for if it waits; for asking for a decision and showing the consequence of each answer, or making the cost of delay explicit.
- DE-06 | slide | ask-and-actions | The decision needed in a callout on the left, rows of action, timing and owner on the right; for closing with the ask and who does what by when, or any decision followed by a short action list.
- DE-07 | slide | ask-and-timeline | A starting point in a callout over a five-step timeline with dates and owners; for showing the sequence of work that follows a decision, or a plan where order and dates matter more than detail.
- BK-01 | slide | answer-with-bars | The answer as the headline; the question, a short answer and horizontal bars below; for answering a question the audience is likely to ask, or holding supporting evidence without lengthening the main story.
- BK-02 | slide | heatmap-callout | One callout sentence over a small heatmap grid with one diagonal highlighted; for showing a pattern across two dimensions, such as period by age, or pointing at one part of a grid of figures.

## Charts (`charts/`)

- CT-02 | chart | bars-ranked | Horizontal bars sorted largest first, the leader in blue, values at the bar ends; for ranking items when the labels are long, or showing which item drives a result.
- CT-03 | chart | line-event | Two lines over time, the one that matters in blue, both ends labelled, an event marked with a dashed line; for showing the effect of a change against a group that did not change, or tying a movement to the moment that caused it.
- CT-04 | chart | line-fit | Actual line in blue with a dashed grey fitted line, both ends labelled; for showing a steady rate of change, or showing how closely actuals follow a fitted or expected path.
- CT-05 | chart | paired-bars | Two bars per category (expected in light grey, actual darker), the category that matters in blue, legend at top left; for comparing actual with expected across a few groups, or showing where two measures agree and where they part.
- CT-06 | chart | waterfall | Vertical bridge from a starting total to an ending total; totals dark grey, the step that matters blue, other steps light grey; for bridging from a starting figure to an ending figure by cause, or showing which driver accounts for most of a change.
- CT-07 | chart | target-tick-bars | Horizontal bars with a dark tick marking each item's target, the miss in blue with its value inside the bar; for showing performance against a target or plan for each item, or picking out the one item that missed.
- CT-08 | chart | dumbbell | Two dots per item joined by a short bar, the earlier point light grey, the later dark, the item that matters in blue; for showing movement between two points for several items at once, or showing which items moved most and in which direction.
- CT-09 | chart | stacked-share | Two 100% stacked horizontal bars, the part that changed in blue with its label inside, the other parts grey; for showing how the make-up of a total shifted between two points, or showing that a result changed because the mix changed.
- CT-10 | chart | heatmap-grid | A small grid of figures on light grey cells, the cells that matter in blue with white text; for showing a pattern across two dimensions, such as period by age, or pointing at one part of a grid of figures.
- CT-11 | chart | range-bar | One low-to-high grey bar with the central figure as a dashed line and the chosen figure as a solid blue line; for showing where a chosen figure sits within a reasonable range, or making the size of the uncertainty visible.
