# Design library index

Reusable design items by type. Descriptions only: plan from this page, and let the builder or design subagent open only the items it names.

- Pick by "Good for", not by subject. Sample subject matter is placeholder.
- Path to a slide: its group folder plus its name: `CP-01 slides/compare/slides.html#/paired-bars-commentary`. Other items: `<folder>/<name>.md` (themes: `themes/look.css`).
- Browse: open `index.html`. Add an item: `README.md`.
- Generated by `build-index.js` from the item files. Do not edit by hand.

## Slides

One sample slide per component, grouped by what the slide has to do. Look: `slides/styles.css`.

### Open and frame

Set up what the audience is about to hear. Folder: `slides/frame`

| Code | Name | Looks like | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FR-01 | `title` | Large headline, kicker above, one line below. No visual. | • Opening with the answer or the subject<br>• Telling the audience who is presenting and when |
| FR-02 | `section-divider` | Section name large, one line saying what the section will show. | • Marking a change of topic in a longer deck<br>• Giving the audience a breath before a new line of argument |
| FR-03 | `agenda-rows` | Numbered rows: part name and one line each. | • Showing the route through a longer deck<br>• Matching what is said to the sections in the bottom bar |
| FR-04 | `goal-three-cards` | Three cards side by side, the third tinted. | • Reminding the audience of the original goal<br>• Laying out problem, action and measure of success in one view<br>• Any three parallel ideas where the last one matters most |
| FR-05 | `statement-quote` | One sentence or quote in large type, source line under it. | • Making the audience hear one message with nothing competing<br>• Letting a customer or stakeholder say it in their own words |

### Compare two things

| Code | Name | Looks like | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| CP-02 | `bars-target-tick` | Horizontal bars with a tick marking the target for each, the miss highlighted; commentary on the right. | • Showing performance against a target or plan for each item<br>• Picking out the one item that missed |
